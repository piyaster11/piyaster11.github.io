Document checklist

    Approval boundary

    This sample stops at Match, Needs check, or Hold. A person in Accounts Payable resolves an exception. Finance owns any payment release in the payment system (placeholder). This page cannot approve, upload a file, or release payment.

    Source documents

    These previews use the same figures as the result above. Open one to enlarge it. The goods-received preview is delivery evidence, not proof of payment.

    Sample rules

    • The check uses the purchase order, the goods-received note, and the invoice.
    • Sample VAT is 7% of the amount before tax, rounded to the satang. The total is the amount before tax plus that VAT.
    • The sample price threshold is 1%. A unit price more than 1% away from the purchase order is Needs check. A gap of 1% or less can still Match when every other rule passes.
    • Quantity billed beyond quantity received is always Hold, even when the unit price matches and the VAT arithmetic is consistent.
    • Match is not payment approval. A goods-received note and a delivery photo show that goods arrived. They are not proof of payment.

    Worked sample totals

    • Match, INV-SAMPLE-1001: 80 × THB 125.00 plus 40 × THB 50.00 is THB 12,000.00 before tax. VAT THB 840.00. Invoice total THB 12,840.00.
    • Needs check, INV-SAMPLE-1002: 50 × THB 203.00 is THB 10,150.00 before tax. VAT THB 710.50. Invoice total THB 10,860.50. The order is 50 × THB 200.00, total THB 10,700.00. The price gap is 1.50%.
    • Hold, INV-SAMPLE-1003: the invoice is 120 × THB 80.00 = THB 9,600.00 before tax, VAT THB 672.00, total THB 10,272.00. Goods received and the purchase order are 100 × THB 80.00, total THB 8,560.00.
